Feature Guide

Complete guide on how every feature works in Billy777 WhatsApp CRM

1 Getting Started — Connect WhatsApp

Step 1: Start the server with node server.js from the project folder.

Step 2: Open http://localhost:3000 in your browser.

Step 3: A QR code will appear automatically. Open WhatsApp on your phone → Settings → Linked Devices → Link a Device → Scan the QR.

Step 4: Once scanned, the bottom-left dot turns green and shows "Connected".

2 Team Inbox — Chat with Contacts

Chat List (Left Panel): Shows all your WhatsApp conversations. Each contact displays their name, last message, timestamp, status badge, and unread count.

Filter Tabs: Filter contacts by status — All, New, Contacted, Interested.

Search: Type in the search bar to find contacts by name, phone number, or last message.

Chat Panel (Center): Click any contact to open their full chat. Messages appear in real-time with WhatsApp-style bubbles. Use the ← back button to return to the list.

Send Messages: Type your message and click Send (or press Enter). Character limit is 4,096.

Quick Replies: Click the ⚡ bolt icon to show saved quick reply buttons above the input — click one to instantly send it.

Status Dropdown: Change a contact's stage (New → Contacted → Interested) from the chat header dropdown.

Contact Info (Right Panel): Click the ℹ️ icon to open/close the contact detail panel. Edit name, phone, email, company, source, tags, and notes. Click "Save Changes".

Schedule Message: Click the 🕐 clock icon to schedule a message for a specific date/time.

3 Contacts — Manage Your Database

Contacts Table: View all contacts in a table with name, phone, status, source, tags, and last active time.

Stats Row: Quick overview showing Total contacts, New, Contacted, Interested, and Sold counts.

Import CSV: Click "Import CSV" → Paste CSV data in the format: phone,name,email,company,tags → Click "Import".

Export CSV: Click "Export" to download all contacts as a CSV file.

Click any row to jump directly to that contact's chat in the Team Inbox.

4 Broadcasts — Bulk Messaging Campaigns

Create Campaign: Click "New Broadcast" → Enter a campaign name, select target audience (All Contacts, New Leads, Contacted, Interested, or Sold), then write your message.

Send: After creating, click the green "Send" button on the campaign card. Messages are sent with random delays between each recipient to reduce the risk of blocks.

Track Progress: Each card shows status (Draft / Sending / Completed / Failed), recipient count, and delivered/failed stats.

Delete: Click "Delete" on any campaign card to remove it.

⚠️ Tip: Avoid sending to very large groups at once. The built-in delays help, but sending natural, relevant messages is the best protection.

5 Automation — Auto-Reply Rules

Create Rule: Click "New Rule" → Enter a name, trigger type, priority, trigger value, and reply message.

Trigger Types:

  • Contains Keyword: Triggers if the incoming message contains any of the comma-separated keywords.
  • Exact Match: Triggers only if the message exactly matches the keyword.
  • First Message: Triggers automatically when a new contact sends their very first message.
  • Regex: Triggers if the message matches a regular expression pattern.

Priority: When multiple rules match, the one with the highest priority number fires first.

Toggle On/Off: Use the toggle switch on each rule card to enable/disable it without deleting.

Edit/Delete: Click "Edit" to modify or "Delete" to remove a rule.

6 Analytics — Dashboard & Insights

Stat Cards: Quick overview of Total Leads, New, Sold, Messages Today, Incoming Today, and Total Revenue.

Lead Pipeline: Visual bar chart showing how many contacts are in each stage — New, Contacted, Interested, Sold, Lost.

Message Trends: Bar chart of messages over the last 7 days.

All data refreshes automatically every 5 seconds.

7 Scheduled Messages — Send Later

How to Schedule: Open any chat in the Inbox → Click the 🕐 clock icon → Write your message, pick a date/time → Click "Schedule".

View All: The Scheduled page lists all pending messages with the recipient, message preview, and scheduled time.

Cancel: Click "Cancel" on any scheduled message to remove it before it sends.

Auto-Send: The server checks every 30 seconds for due messages and sends them automatically.

Status: Shows "pending" until sent, then "sent". Failed messages show "failed".

8 Quick Replies — Canned Responses

Create: Click "New Quick Reply" → Enter a title, shortcut (e.g. /welcome), and the reply message.

Use in Chat: In the Team Inbox, click the ⚡ bolt icon to reveal your quick reply buttons. Click one to instantly send it.

Edit: Click any card on this page to edit it.

Delete: Click "Delete" on the card to remove it.

9 Settings

Connection: View current WhatsApp connection status — green dot means connected, red means disconnected.

Show QR Code: Click "Show QR Code" to re-display the QR if you need to re-link your device.

Theme: Click the moon 🌙 icon in the top bar to toggle between Dark Mode and Light Mode.

About: Shows the app version and how long the server has been running.

💡 Tips & Best Practices

Auto-Refresh: The app polls every 5 seconds for new messages and connection status — no manual refresh needed.

Tags: Use comma-separated tags on contacts (e.g. "vip, premium, follow-up") for easy organization and filtering.

Phone Detection: Click "Detect" in the contact info panel to auto-resolve the real phone number from WhatsApp.

Revenue Tracking: Set revenue on Sold contacts to track total revenue across Analytics and the Contacts page.

Broadcasts + Automation: Combine them — use Auto-Reply to handle first responses, and Broadcasts for follow-up campaigns.
